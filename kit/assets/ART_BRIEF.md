# Art and audio brief

Style: cozy illustrated 2D night-time bedroom, clean silhouettes, soft paper-like shading, cool blue room with warm small light accents, gentle physical comedy. The game must remain readable on a phone. Interactive props have higher contrast than decoration. No text baked into artwork.

Generate/commission a style sheet first: character proportions, room palette, prop line weight, consistent light direction, calm/stirring/near-wake expressions. Approve that sheet before a full set. Use separate transparent sprites with the asset manifest's pivots and named states. Source resolutions are proposed authoring sizes; logical collision geometry stays in level data.

Reusable asset prompt: “Create [asset_id] for this approved Hush style sheet. Orthographic 2D mobile-game prop, consistent light and outline, transparent background, no lettering. Supply the following states separately: [states]. Preserve silhouette and pivot between frames. Keep fine detail secondary to clear recognition at small size.”

Character motion: sleepy rather than injured; no horror. Cup wobble must reveal progress, train movement must reveal the carriage anchor, and the window cue must reveal the gust before noise starts. Socket highlights are rendered in code, so their positions exactly match model data.

Audio source inventory: gentle scrapes, small padded catch, distinct hard crash, squeaky duck, quiet wheels, release click, rattle, creak/slam, breathing/snore, UI taps and soft success. Mix on an actual phone speaker and headphones. No mechanic depends on hearing. Avoid excessive voice count by coalescing continuous sources.

For every final asset retain creator/source, date, license or permission, modifications and original file. Replace ambiguous assets before release. This kit includes no finished production art or audio; the prototype screenshots are reference evidence only.
