# Store handoff

Verify current Google/Apple requirements from official sources. Prepare signed packages using authorized secure credentials, screenshots from the release build, descriptions, icon, ratings, privacy statements, support and credits. Obtain owner decisions for final name, IDs, price and countries when those actually block packaging. Prepare the complete reviewable candidate before asking for final submission approval.

## Gate

Concrete release candidate, validated signing/manifest, truthful store materials and checklist. Do not publish without owner authorization. If a credential or device is missing, identify it precisely and finish all unaffected preparation.

Follow WORKING_AGREEMENT.md. End the phase with exact tests/results, files changed, unresolved blockers and next step. Update docs/STATUS.md.
