# Formative phone playtest

Purpose: evaluate the three-room vertical slice, not certify commercial demand. Recruit ten first-time testers with a mix of casual-game experience. Use the same build and record actual phone model/OS. Test one at a time. Ask permission before recording observations or input replays; do not collect unnecessary personal data.

Opening script: “Try these three short rooms. You can stop whenever you want. I am testing the game, not you.” Do not explain the solution or point to the right object. If the person asks for help, record it, then let the in-game hint teach them.

For each tester record an anonymous participant ID, game experience, device/OS, build, room, mode, attempts, time to first meaningful action, first completion, hints, voluntary retries, alternate strategy attempts, and any input trouble. Write down their exact explanation of the last failure. Note when they stop and ask why without persuading them to continue.

After the slice ask: “How much do you want to play another room, from 1 to 5?” Then ask what felt satisfying and what felt unfair. Do not suggest the answers.

Initial gates from BRD: 8/10 clear Room 1 within three attempts without coaching; 8/10 explain their failure; 6/10 voluntarily retry the hard room; 3/10 intentionally try another solution; 7/10 rate wanting another room at least 4/5. Show both counts and notes. These are small-sample design signals, not retention or revenue predictions.

Decision: proceed, iterate, or revisit mechanic. Name the one or two largest observed problems, the proposed change and the next test. Keep prior results when a build changes. Do not merge unrelated builds into one result without disclosure.
